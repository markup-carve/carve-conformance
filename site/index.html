    What this does not show

    • The models are partial. A theorem follows from its definitions. No proof yet connects the full parsers in JavaScript, PHP or Rust to the model.
    • Fixtures are finite. Agreement on these cases is evidence, not a guarantee for every input.
    • Adapters cover subsets. A foreign format passing here means the measured fixtures map cleanly, not that any document in that format fits Carve.
